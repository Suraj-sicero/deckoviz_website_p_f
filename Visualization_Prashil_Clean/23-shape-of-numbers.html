The Ulam Prime Spiral
Discovered by Stanislaw Ulam during a boring 1963 conference: writing integers in a square spiral reveals that prime numbers unexpectedly line up along prominent diagonal lines.
💡 These diagonal lines correspond to quadratic polynomials like $f(n) = 4n^2 + bn + c$ that generate unusually dense sequences of primes.
