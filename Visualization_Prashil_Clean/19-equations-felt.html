Mass-Energy Equivalence
Mass is simply concentrated energy. Because the speed of light squared (c²) is an astronomical number (~9 × 10¹⁶ m²/s²), even a tiny drop of mass contains enormous latent energy.
💡 Manipulate mass to see how a single gram yields energy equal to 21 kilotons of TNT.
