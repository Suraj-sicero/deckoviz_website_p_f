H₂O — Water
Water (H₂O) — The Universal Solvent
Water’s oxygen atom pulls shared electrons strongly, creating a bent 104.5° polar molecule with positive and negative ends. This polarity allows water to dissolve nutrients and form open hex-grid hydrogen bonds when freezing.
💡 Structure → Function: Because ice forms an open hexagonal lattice, solid ice is 9% less dense than liquid water—causing ice to float and insulating marine life beneath frozen lakes.
