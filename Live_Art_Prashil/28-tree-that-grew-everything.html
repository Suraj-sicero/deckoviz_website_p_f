LUCA — Last Universal Common Ancestor (~4.0 Billion Years Ago)
The single-celled organism from which all Bacteria, Archaea, and Eukarya descended. Every creature living today shares LUCA’s universal genetic code (DNA/RNA) and ATP energy currency.
